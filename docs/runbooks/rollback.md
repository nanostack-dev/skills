# Recover from a faulty skill revision

Identify the source commit and affected consumers. Revert the faulty instructions/resources through a focused PR. For a pinned consumer, restore its previously verified source pin and rebuild its managed catalog through that consumer's supported installer.

Verify discovery, invocation policy and the affected procedure against a disposable concrete case. Existing generated reports and external actions are not undone by reverting the skill; handle any resulting recovery with the owning project and its authorization boundary.

Keep evidence, contributing causes and prevention in the appropriate troubleshooting, ADR or postmortem record.
